# TripWise Launch Video Plan

## What is it?
TripWise: AI-powered travel planning for India with AWS Bedrock integration. Plan trips that actually fit your budget using real-time prices and intelligent optimization.

## Key beats
1. **Hook**: Budget-breaking dream trips → TripWise solving it
2. **Real prices**: Live flight/bus/train/hotel scraping
3. **AI magic**: AWS Bedrock copilot optimizing your budget
4. **Result**: Perfect trip within budget

## Tone
**Polished** - Professional AWS Hackathon showcase with clean, confident energy

## Visual Identity
- **Font**: DM Sans (from project)
- **Colors**: 
  - Green: #245b3f (trust, money, success)
  - Dark: #19452f
  - Background: #f5f7f4
  - Surface: #ffffff
- **Style**: Clean UI showcase, smooth animations

## Storyboard (20 seconds @ 120 BPM)

### Scene 1: Hook (0-3s) - Beats 1-6
**Visual**: Text "₹20,000 budget" appears, then dream destinations flash by getting more expensive
**Text**: "Dream trip: ₹45,000" in red
**Music**: Tension build

### Scene 2: Solution Arrives (3-7s) - Beats 7-14
**Visual**: TripWise logo animates in with AWS Bedrock badge
**Text**: "TripWise + AWS Bedrock"
**Music**: Resolution, energy up

### Scene 3: Real Prices (7-12s) - Beats 15-24
**Visual**: Real scraped prices loading in: Flights ₹6,800 | Buses ₹1,200 | Hotels ₹8,500/night
**Text**: "Real-time prices. Real decisions."
**Music**: Steady, confident

### Scene 4: AI Copilot (12-17s) - Beats 25-34
**Visual**: Chat interface: "Find cheaper hotel" → AI responds with alternative, budget goes green
**Text**: "AWS Bedrock AI: Your travel copilot"
**Music**: Peak energy

### Scene 5: Outro (17-20s) - Beats 35-40
**Visual**: Final budget breakdown in green, all categories under budget
**Text**: "TripWise. Smart travel for real budgets."
**Sub**: "AWS Hackathon 2026 | github.com/[your-repo]"
**Music**: Satisfying resolve

## Music
- **Tempo**: 120 BPM (2 beats per second)
- **Style**: Upbeat tech/corporate
- **Key**: C major (bright, optimistic)
- Simple electronic drums + bass + synth melody
